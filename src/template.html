Where the names fall

One dot for each person a book names, in order of first mention. Pick a name anywhere on the page and its thread is drawn through the books.

Everyone named in two or more books. Numbers count the verses that use the name.

Scripture text is the World English Bible, which is in the public domain. WEB follows the Byzantine Majority Text, so a few names differ from editions based on the critical text: Justus for Titius Justus, Amplias for Ampliatus, Nymphas for Nympha, Joses for Joseph.

A verse counts only when it uses the person's name. Jesus, figures from Israel's scriptures, and unnamed people such as Paul's nephew are left out. People who share a name are separate entries.

Pick a name to read every verse that mentions it, who is named beside it, and who it might be identified with.
